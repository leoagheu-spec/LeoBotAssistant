LEOBOTASSIST - HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE (FREE) AND SHARE IT
=======================================================

You do this ONCE. Afterwards you just send the link to your friends.

IMPORTANT: Don't double-click the .js files and don't use "drag and drop" hosting.
The AI brain (netlify/functions/ask.js) only runs when the site is published from GitHub (or the Netlify CLI).

STEP 1 - Get the free AI key (2 minutes)
  1. Go to  https://aistudio.google.com/apikey  and sign in with Google.
  2. Click "Create API key" and copy it. Keep it secret. Never put it in the app files.

STEP 2 - Put the files on GitHub (5 minutes)
  1. Make a free account at https://github.com
  2. Click "+" (top right) > "New repository". Name it  leobotassist . Click "Create repository".
  3. Click "uploading an existing file". Unzip LeoBotAssist.zip on your PC and drag EVERYTHING inside the
     LeoBotAssist folder into the page (index.html, app.js, sw.js, manifest.json, the icons, netlify.toml
     and the folder  netlify ). Click "Commit changes".
     Check that the folder netlify/functions/ask.js shows up in the repository.

STEP 3 - Publish it on Netlify (3 minutes)
  1. Make a free account at https://app.netlify.com (sign in with GitHub).
  2. "Add new project" > "Import an existing project" > GitHub > pick  leobotassist .
  3. Leave every setting as it is and click Deploy.
  4. Open Project configuration > Environment variables > Add a variable:
        Key:   GEMINI_API_KEY
        Value: (paste your key)
  5. Go to Deploys > Trigger deploy > Deploy project without cache.

STEP 4 - Check that the brain works
  Open this in your browser (use your own site name):
      https://YOUR-SITE.netlify.app/.netlify/functions/ask
  You should see:   {"ok":true,"brain":"online"}
  If it says "missing GEMINI_API_KEY", redo step 3.4 and 3.5.

STEP 5 - Share with friends
  Send them your link  https://YOUR-SITE.netlify.app  (or tap "Share with friends" inside the app).

STEP 6 - Make it a real app
  A) Install from the browser (free, takes 5 seconds, no store):
     Android (Chrome): open the link > tap "Install app" on the first screen (or Chrome menu > Install app).
     iPhone (Safari):  Share button > Add to Home Screen.
     Windows/Mac PC (Chrome or Edge): install icon at the right of the address bar.
  B) A downloadable .apk file you can send to friends:
     1. Go to https://www.pwabuilder.com and paste your link.
     2. Click Package for stores > Android > Generate. Download the zip.
     3. Inside is an .apk (install directly) and an .aab (for Google Play).
     4. Send the .apk to your friends. On Android they open it and allow "install unknown apps" for their browser or file manager.
  C) Google Play (costs a one-time $25): upload the .aab from PWABuilder in Google Play Console.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  - "Brain had a hiccup" in the app: open the link from step 4. If it doesn't say online, the key is missing or the deploy is old.
  - Google's free AI has daily limits. If it says "busy", wait a bit. The app tries 3 different free models before giving up.
  - Questions are counted on each phone. Clearing the app data resets the daily count.
